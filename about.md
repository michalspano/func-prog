I plan to write about functional programming on this subdomain. I'm very fond
of it, as you may know by now. It's also about another way of thinking and
approaching problems. I recommend the following books and/or articles:

* [The Haskell Road to Logic, Math and Programming][doets-eijck]
* [Programming in Haskell][hutton]

I read lots of blogs on FP; I recommend [this][why-haskell] one on Hacker news
for an interesting deep-dive. My FP journey has been inspired by the one and
only [*Alexey Kutepov*][tsoding] a.k.a. Tsoding/rexim.
> Watch [this][tsoding-yt] video in particular; it's about `GHC` and FFI with
> C. Also, [this][tsoding-yt2] video is too interesting, and funny too!

The following lecture series by Brian Beckman are too interesting; I liked
[Don't fear the Monad][beckman], [The Zen of Stateless State - The State
Monad][beckman2]. I continually discover new lecture, video series which I'll
keep writing about here.

Lastly, we prove the haters wrong; Monad is more than just *a monoid in the
category of endofunctors*. Rexim's video on [IO monad][what-is-io-monad] is a
must to watch!
